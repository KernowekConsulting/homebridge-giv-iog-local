# GivHome 4.2.0

## Two batteries. One home.

GivHome now brings the **Marstek Venus E 3.0** into Apple Home alongside a compatible **GivEnergy All in One and Gateway**. See each battery’s charge level, or check **Battery SOC** to see how full your combined storage is.

Set **Night Target** once for both batteries. GivHome can charge them together during **Cheap Overnight** and coordinate them during **Intelligent Octopus Go Smart Windows**, including Grace Period protection. In Eco, Venus can help meet sustained household demand while the All in One responds to faster changes.

Use **Charge To** and **Export To** in Apple Home to set a combined battery percentage. To get started, open **Marstek Integration** in Homebridge settings and follow the [Venus setup guide](docs/MARSTEK_SETUP.md). GivEnergy-only homes can carry on with their existing setup.

Charging controls and Intelligent Octopus Go battery protection also work more reliably on supported **GivEnergy AC-coupled inverters**.
